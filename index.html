Our Story

A Legacy in Natural Stone Since 1996

Mount Exports is an Indian natural-stone business focused on marble, granite, blocks and slabs for buyers and architectural projects. Our collection highlights the natural movement, colour, texture and character that make each stone distinctive. With an export-oriented approach and a worldwide enquiry focus, we connect stone requirements with suitable material selections.

Worldwide shipping is offered as part of our export service. Enquiries are handled directly with the business — from product shortlisting through to the discussion of supply and destination-specific requirements.
