GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions.

What types of natural stone do you offer?

Mount Exports presents marble and granite, with block and slab formats discussed according to the buyer’s requirements.

Can I enquire about a specific product?

Yes. Select the product or describe the stone type you are interested in, and share any relevant dimensions, finish and quantity.

Are the images an exact representation of available stock?

Images are representative of stone types. Natural stone varies, and current selection, dimensions and finish should be confirmed directly.

Do you handle international export enquiries?

Yes, international buyers can share their destination and requirements so the relevant details can be discussed directly.

What details should I include in an enquiry?

Include your name, country or destination, product interest, quantity, dimensions, finish and project details where available.
